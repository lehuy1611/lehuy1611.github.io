About Me

Xin chào ! I'm Huy, Research Master's student at and the University of Montreal , advised by Aishwarya Agrawal. These days, I'm broadly interested in capturing transferable knowledge from internet-scale videos and grounding it in embodied agents that can generalize and act across diverse environments.

In my previous chapter, I earned my B.Eng. from International University – Vietnam National University HCMC in 2024. During my undergrad, I was fortunate to work as AI Engineer at VNG Corporation and AI Research Resident at FPT Software AI Center. My undergrad team was one of the winners at CVPR 2023 NVIDIA AI City Challenge.

Research Interests

My research centers on Vision-Language, Video Representation, and World Modeling — unified by a common goal: building Embodied Agents that can Perceive, Reason, Plan, and Act across diverse environments over long horizons. Previously, I worked on Intelligent Surveillance Systems, Video-Language Understanding, Object-Centric Representation, and Robotic Learning.
